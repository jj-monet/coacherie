Guided Reflection Journal

This isn't a journal that hands you the same five questions every day. Each entry opens with a short check-in, then a single prompt drawn from six different corners of reflection — values, transitions, relationships, body, the things you tend to avoid, and the small good things too.

Answer as much or as little as you want, tag what it was about, and the next prompt shifts to meet you there — quietly making sure nothing gets circled forever, and nothing gets left untouched too long either.

There's no AI reading what you write, no algorithm interpreting you. Just a simple, steady logic that keeps things moving — and your words, staying exactly where you put them.
